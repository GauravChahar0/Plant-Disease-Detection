Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers on PlantGuard AI diagnostic precision, photo capturing guidelines, and organic plant health treatments.

Our deep convolutional neural network (MobileNetV2 architecture with transfer learning) achieves an exceptional 95% to 99.2% benchmark accuracy across 27 distinct plant disease categories and healthy baselines.

The model was trained and cross-validated on more than 54,000 expert-labeled leaf images, specifically weighted to handle varied lighting, angles, and early-stage symptom detection.

To obtain maximum diagnostic certainty, follow these golden rules:

  • Natural diffused daylight: Shoot in soft morning or overcast light. Avoid harsh direct sun reflections or intense camera flash.
  • Fill the frame: The infected leaf blade should fill at least 60%–80% of the picture.
  • Focus sharply on lesions: Tap your screen to lock focus on discoloration, powdery spots, or wilting leaf margins.
  • Clean background: Hold a neutral hand or piece of cardboard behind the leaf if background foliage is distracting.

Yes, 100%! The web application is engineered from the ground up for mobile devices with instant camera capture support on both iOS (Safari) and Android (Chrome).

Additionally, you can download our dedicated PlantGuard Mobile App (APK / PWA) from the link beside the Plants section, giving you rapid one-tap diagnoses while inspecting orchards or farms.

Every single diagnosed condition provides actionable management guidance tailored for both home gardeners and organic farmers:

  • Biological controls: Recommendations such as Bacillus subtilis beneficial bacteria, copper octanoate sprays, and botanical Neem oil extracts.
  • Cultural practices: Sanitization methods, targeted pruning, air circulation tips, and drip irrigation practices to halt fungal spore transmission.
  • Commercial options: Chemical fungicide guidelines when intervention is required for large acreages.

Your privacy is fully protected. Images uploaded for disease scanning are processed strictly in temporary runtime memory and are never retained, sold, or shared with third parties.

Diagnostic logs and scan records reside solely inside your personal browser's LocalStorage, meaning you retain complete ownership and can clear your history anytime with zero footprints left behind.

PlantGuard AI currently covers Apple, Cherry, Corn (Maize), Grape, Peach, Pepper (Bell), Potato, Strawberry, and Tomato.

Supported conditions include Early Blight, Late Blight, Bacterial Spot, Powdery Mildew, Black Rot, Esca, Cedar Rust, Common Rust, Gray Leaf Spot, Northern Leaf Blight, Leaf Scorch, alongside verified healthy controls for every crop.
